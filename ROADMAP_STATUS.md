# Stable-reduction roadmap audit

Specification: [TauCetiRoadmap PR #142](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/pull/142),
`TauCetiRoadmap/StableReduction/README.md` on the PR branch.

## Completion verdict

**Not complete.** The project builds, but a successful build is only an integration check. Several
roadmap summits are currently fields of `*Engine` or `*Data` structures rather than theorems
constructed from the roadmap hypotheses. In particular, there is no unconditional classical or
pointed stable-reduction theorem, no construction of family stabilization, and no construction of
the coherent-cohomology, duality, Picard, resolution, or contraction inputs used by those results.

Status labels:

- **done**: the requested construction and theorem are present, not merely accepted as input;
- **partial**: some genuine definitions or lemmas are present, but a named conclusion is missing;
- **deferred**: the conclusion is a field of a supplier/engine structure;
- **absent**: no implementation of the named target was found.

1. Finish Layers 0–3 without theorem-package fields: differentials/Fitting/singular loci,
   normalization, coherent cohomology, duality, line-bundle operations, ampleness, descent, and
   the single-marking contraction.

   contraction results, then derive minimal models instead of accepting engines.

   reduction in all genera.

   and finish the geometric worked examples.

`StabilizationEngine`, or `StableGluingEngine` is needed as a hypothesis of an end-goal theorem.
